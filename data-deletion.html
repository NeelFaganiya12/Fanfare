Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 2026

You can remove your data from Fanfare at any time using the options below. Fanfare is operated by 1001751772 ONTARIO INC., Ontario, Canada.

Disconnect a social account

To revoke access to a connected Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn account and delete its stored access token, open Social Platforms in the app and click Disconnect on that account. The token is removed immediately.

You can also remove Fanfare directly from your Facebook Business Integrations settings.

Delete your entire account and data

To permanently delete your Fanfare account and all associated data, including your profile, generated content, uploaded images, and connected-account tokens, email superadmin@thefanfare.ca from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.
