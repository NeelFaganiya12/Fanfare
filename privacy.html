Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fanfare ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our marketing content platform.

Who we are

Fanfare is operated by 1001751772 ONTARIO INC., Ontario, Canada, which is responsible for the information described in this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Business profile: details you provide such as brokerage/company, market, and brand voice.
  • Content you create: inputs you enter, reference links you supply, images you upload, and the marketing content we generate for you.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn, we store an encrypted access token and basic account identifiers so we can publish on your behalf at your request.
  • Usage data: basic logs needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

How we use information

  • To generate marketing content and images you request.
  • To publish or schedule posts to the social accounts you connect, only when you initiate it.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service and provide support.

Meta Platform data

When you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram Business account, we access only the permissions required to publish content (instagram_content_publish, Page publishing, and related read permissions). We use this access solely to publish content you create. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. You can revoke access at any time from within the app or from your Facebook Business Integrations settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the platform (e.g. hosting, AI generation, and image storage), and with the social platforms you explicitly choose to publish to.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. AI-generated images are an exception: they are kept for 30 days and then permanently deleted, so download anything you want to keep. Access tokens are removed when you disconnect an account. You can request full deletion at any time (see our Data Deletion instructions).

Security

Social access tokens are encrypted at rest, and passwords are stored only as secure hashes. We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
